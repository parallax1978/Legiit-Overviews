## Fixed monthly costs not in the table

- Vercel Pro $20, DataForSEO prepaid ($50 minimum top-up), Inngest Pro $99 once past 50,000 executions/month, Neon from $0, Firecrawl Hobby $16, Resend/PostHog/Sentry free tiers, Cloudflare R2 under $5 at 1,000 queries.

## What the numbers say

- Capture is cheap; extraction is the lever. Every extra render per day costs about $0.002 to scrape but about $0.005 to $0.016 to extract, so extraction must run only on unique content (hash plus near-duplicate check) and on the cheap tier.
- A Pro tier at 50 queries with 6 renders/day is marginal on Haiku and loss-making on Sonnet; 30 queries at 4 renders/day keeps a healthy margin. Make cadence a visible tier feature.
- The Agency tier is only viable at Starter cadence by default; sell multi-render fidelity as a per-query add-on priced at cost plus margin.
- The free trial costs well under a dollar per signup when the LLM analysis is delayed to day 3 and limited to three cited pages.
