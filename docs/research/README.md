# Research

Research for the AI Overview tracking and citation reverse-engineering SaaS, gathered on 2026-10-06.

Method: seven researchers (one per dimension) worked from primary sources (vendor docs, pricing pages, Google documentation, published studies) and returned structured findings with URLs. The three most plan-critical claims per dimension were then handed to an independent fact-checker instructed to refute them, and a final completeness critic reviewed everything for gaps and contradictions. Verdicts are recorded in each file; a `partially_wrong` verdict means the corrected wording in the fact-check table supersedes the original claim.

Prices, limits and version numbers are as of 2026-10-06 and will drift. Re-verify anything marked critical before relying on it.
