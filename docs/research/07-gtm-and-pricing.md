# Go-to-market and pricing

_Research dimension: Go-to-market and pricing_

Gathered 2026-10-06. See [README.md](README.md) for method and caveats.

## Summary

As of 2026-10-06 the AI-visibility tracking category has converged on a clear price norm: roughly $1.20–$2.00 per tracked prompt per month for daily, multi-LLM tracking (Mentions.so $49/25 prompts, $99/50, $199/100, $399/300; Otterly $29/15, $189/100, $489/400; Peec €89/50 and €199/150 on its own comparison page, $95/$245/$495 on GetApp; Rankscale $99 for 1,200 credits), while usage-priced ZipTie sets the floor at "$0.01" per prompt-engine-check and Ahrefs sells prompt checks at $0.010–$0.020 each. Incumbent SEO suites now bundle Google AI Overview tracking into core plans (Semrush Starter $199/mo with 50 prompts daily; SE Ranking Core $129/mo with 100 prompts daily plus an $89/mo AI Search add-on; Ahrefs Lite $129/mo with only 5 tracked AI prompts), so a new entrant cannot win on "another dashboard" and must win on the output: a 7-day pattern report and a buildable page brief for ONE query, fulfilled by freelancers. Google reports AI Overviews at "over 2 billion monthly users" (July 2025), Pew measured AI summaries on 18% of searches with clicks on organic results falling from 15% to 8% and only 1% of visits clicking a link inside the summary, and Ahrefs measured a 34.5% CTR drop, which is the buyer's pain. Semrush's study shows AIOs are overwhelmingly informational (76–80%) and under 3% transactional, so the thread's "buying-intent query that already triggers an AI Overview" is scarce and the product needs an AIO-trigger check at onboarding. The buyers with proven willingness to pay are agencies (Mentions.so's white-label Agency tier, Peec's agency bundles of 111–722 prompts, SE Ranking's $69 Agency Pack, ZipTie's "agencies with a client roster" positioning) and in-house B2B marketers (Profound cites "a third of the Fortune 100"); local businesses and SEO freelancers are reachable mainly through Legiit itself. Legiit (launched Feb 7 2018, bootstrapped, 45 staff, "over one million orders", "25,000+ freelancers", average order ~$90, top buyers ~$30,000/month) already sells adjacent SaaS: Command Center at $19/mo with a 7-day trial, Legiit Leads at $47–$497/mo whose every tier includes "AI visibility check + Google AI Overview verdicts", and a GEO marketplace category; six of Legiit's fifteen best-selling SEO services in September 2026 were explicitly AIO/GEO-themed, which is direct demand evidence from the founder's own audience. The category's free-hook norm is a 7-day, no-card trial with 25–50 prompts (Profound's trial is literally "50 prompts run daily for 7 days", ZipTie 25 daily prompts, Otterly 50 prompts), plus free no-signup checkers (Ahrefs AI Overviews Tracker, HubSpot AI Search Grader, SE Ranking 5 checks/day) used as lead magnets. Benchmarks for the trial funnel are 18.2% opt-in trial-to-paid, 48.8% card-required, 2.6% freemium (First Page Sage, Sept 2025). Product Hunt is hit-or-miss in this niche (Otterly #2 of the day with 108 upvotes; Rankscale 3 points), whereas SEO newsletters have reliable reach (SEOFOMO 45,000+ subscribers, 45% open, 10% click; Growth Memo 29,000+). Recommendation: position "Legiit Overviews" as the only tool that turns one AI Overview into a brief and a build, price at Free (1 query, 7 days) / $39 / $99 / $249 anchored just below the $2-per-prompt norm but including briefs, and bundle fulfillment through Legiit gigs.
