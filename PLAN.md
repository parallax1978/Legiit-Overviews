# Legiit Overviews: execution plan

A SaaS that automates Jake Ward's six-step process for winning a Google AI Overview: pick one buying-intent query, capture the AI Overview every day, find the patterns with AI, reverse-engineer the cited pages, build a better page from a brief, then keep tracking. Source thread and requirements: `docs/source-thread.md`. Research behind every decision: `docs/research/`. Cost model: `docs/unit-economics.md`.

Working name: Legiit Overviews (pending counsel; must not contain "Google", "AI Overview(s)", "Gemini" or "SGE").

This plan was produced on 2026-10-06 from fact-checked research (29 research and verification agents) and a judged design panel (three independent proposals, three judges). It is written to be executed by Claude Code one task at a time. See "How to execute" at the end.

---

## 1. What we are building

One query at a time, the product:

1. **Confirms** the query triggers an AI Overview in Google Search for the chosen country, language and device, and suggests sibling queries that do if it does not.
2. **Captures** the AI Overview on a schedule (full answer blocks, the citation chips mapped to references, the organic top 20, timestamp, status) into a global capture pool shared by every customer tracking the same query.
3. **Counts** what keeps showing up: recurring claims, recommended entities versus cited domains, format fingerprint, source survival (CORE / RECURRING / ROTATING), citation stability, semantic drift, share of citations in the organic top 10, and a day-to-day diff. Every number carries its sample size and a confidence label.
4. **Reverse-engineers** the most-cited displaceable pages: words before the first direct answer, heading outline, tables, lists, comparison blocks, numeric density, schema.org types, author, dates, word count, links, FAQ, plus LLM-tagged topics, entities, evidence and questions answered; then a coverage matrix showing what all winners share, what no winner covers, and which AI Overview claims no cited page supports.
5. **Writes a brief** (Claude Opus 5.5): answer lead, must-cover topics and entities with recurrence, required formats, baseline evidence, new-to-cite opportunities, outline, technical checklist, avoid list, with an evidence reference on every item and the EU AI Act "AI-generated draft" marker on export.
6. **Tracks after publish**: whether the customer's URL is cited or brand mentioned, at which match level, its survival over a rolling window, which page sections the citing blocks map to, and first-seen / lost / regained events, with model-regime changes shown as platform events rather than losses.

Positioning: the only tool that turns ONE AI Overview into a page you can rank in it. Price the analysis and the brief, not the scrape.

### Not in v1 (explicitly deferred)

Agency tier, white-label reports, client workspaces and pitch mode (v1.1; schema is prepared for them). Public API, MCP server and public share pages (v1.1, derived data only). Google AI Mode and every non-Google engine. Search Console integration (v1.1; the Generative AI performance report exists since 2026-08-31 worldwide, impressions only, API exposure unverified). LLM-rubric draft scoring (v1 ships the deterministic version). City-level targeting (country-level only; 10 launch countries). Non-English claim clustering as a sold feature (accepted, labelled beta). Self-hosted Google scraping of any kind. Automated Legiit order creation (v1 deep-links to the GEO category and accepts a pasted order reference). PDF exports. Annual billing, Stripe Tax, EU data residency.

## 2. Decisions that are settled (do not re-open while building)

These come from the research and its critique; the rationale is in `docs/research/00-summary.md` and the ADRs in `docs/decisions/`.

1. **Milestone 0 gates the build.** A two-week CLI pilot captures ~50 buying-intent queries, produces the fixture corpus, and sells ~10 Claude-generated playbooks by hand through Legiit. The SaaS build starts only if an AI Overview is present on at least 5 of 7 days for at least 60 percent of queries and at least 5 playbooks are paid for.
2. **Never request google.com.** All capture goes through a `SerpProvider` interface. DataForSEO (Standard queue, `load_async_ai_overview=true`, postback) is primary pending written confirmation on its Terms section 7.1; SerpApi Production ($150/month, Legal Shield; `google` then `google_ai_overview` with the page token redeemed inside the same call within 60 seconds, billed as two searches) is the fallback and the 5 percent second-opinion sample. Provider health scoring switches primary automatically.
3. **Capture is a global pool.** `capture_series` is unique on (normalized_query, gl, hl, location_code, device, provider). Snapshots belong to the series, never to a customer. Tenancy and row-level security apply only to tracked queries, analysis runs, briefs, events and budgets.
4. **Cadence is a tier feature and extraction runs only on unique content.** Starter: 1 render/day desktop. Pro: 3 renders/day on desktop and mobile. Every tier gets 2 renders/day for the first 7 days of a query so the full report gate (at least 5 capture-days and 10 renders) is reachable by day 7. Exact content-hash and a near-duplicate check (embedding cosine at or above 0.98 against the previous render) skip the LLM, so extra renders cost scrape money only.
5. **Snapshot model.** Status enum `present_full | present_collapsed | absent | provider_error | blocked | timeout`; kind enum `text | list | table | product_grid | video | mixed`; blocks with `reference_indexes`; references with normalised URL, registrable domain and platform class; unknown block types preserved as `unknown` with raw text; schema-validation failures feed provider health. Capture day is computed in the series' timezone; a slot is missed after 3 attempts.
6. **Retention.** Raw provider payloads in Cloudflare R2 with a 90-day lifecycle; normalised rows forever; cited-page HTML 14 days; derived features plus at most 5 quotes of at most 200 characters per page forever; AI Overview images never stored; no model training on AI Overview text, ever.
7. **Own-page matching is a standalone tested module** with match levels `exact_url > canonical > path_prefix > subdomain > registrable_domain > brand_entity`; platform domains (youtube, reddit, medium, linkedin, facebook, quora) only match at `exact_url` or `path_prefix`.
8. **Add-query flow.** Normalise (NFKC, trim, collapse whitespace, lowercase, strip trailing punctuation), reject search operators, cap at 200 characters, reject person-name or PII queries with a cheap classifier, run the DataForSEO Labs pre-check (about one cent) for sibling suggestions, confirm with one Live Advanced call, then join or create the series. "No AI Overview yet, watching" is a first-class state with zero LLM spend.
9. **Guardrails ship in the first SaaS sprint.** Per-org capture and LLM budgets checked before enqueue, global per-provider daily ceilings with auto-pause and founder alert, one idempotency key per (series, capture_day, slot) so a fallback can never double-bill, Anthropic and Vercel spend limits, Turnstile plus a disposable-email blocklist, one free series per verified email, no LLM analysis for free accounts before day 3, 3 cited pages on free.
10. **Stack.** pnpm monorepo. `packages/core` (pure TypeScript, no framework imports) is shared by `apps/cli` (Milestone 0) and `apps/web` (Next.js 16 App Router on Vercel Pro). Neon Postgres with pgvector, Drizzle ORM pinned to 0.45.x, Better Auth with the organization and Stripe plugins, Inngest for scheduling (Vercel Workflows is the documented cost fallback), Stripe Billing, Resend plus React Email, PostHog and Sentry, Cloudflare R2. Claude through `@anthropic-ai/sdk` with `zodOutputFormat` structured outputs and the Message Batches API for all scheduled work. Model IDs live only in `packages/core/src/config/models.ts` as aliases (`MODEL_EXTRACT` Haiku-class, `MODEL_MATRIX` = `claude-sonnet-5-5`, `MODEL_BRIEF` = `claude-opus-5-5`); a test fails if a literal `claude-` ID appears anywhere else. Embeddings: voyage-4-lite, 1024 dimensions.
11. **Pin versions on day 0 and avoid same-day releases.** Next.js 16.3.x (16.4.0 shipped on 2026-10-06), TypeScript 6.x, Drizzle 0.45.x. Record the exact versions installed in `CLAUDE.md`. Do not invent patch numbers; take the latest patch of the pinned minor at scaffold time.
12. **Metrics have one definition** (see `docs/spec/metrics.md`): survival buckets are CORE at or above 80 percent, RECURRING 40 to 80 percent, ROTATING under 40 percent of observable renders; confidence labels by sample size are low under 10 renders, medium 10 to 20, high at 21 or more; expanded-only claim clusters use `renders_observable` that excludes `present_collapsed` renders.
13. **Every outward surface is derived data only.** No API, MCP or share page in v1; when they come, contract tests assert no block text and no full reference lists leave the tenant boundary.
14. **Legal surface.** Terms with a rights warranty, no redistribution of raw data, AI-draft disclosure and acceptable-use rules; privacy policy; "tracks AI Overviews in Google Search" wording with the trademark footer; named crawler user agent with a `/bot` page, robots.txt and Content-Signal compliance, at most 1 request per second per host, SSRF protections; a living `docs/legal-risk.md` register.
15. **Jake Ward markets Mentions.so** on his own site. Do not tag him or plan on his amplification; launch on Legiit-owned channels and one SEO newsletter placement.

## 4. Architecture

```
apps/cli  (Milestone 0 pilot, fixtures, evals, ops)      apps/web (Next.js 16 App Router on Vercel Pro)
        \                                                 /   UI, Inngest handler, DataForSEO postback webhook,
         \                                               /    Better Auth routes, Stripe webhook
          +-------------- packages/core -----------------+
            providers/   DataForSEO + SerpApi adapters, health, failover
            intake/      normalise, validate, PII classifier, pre-check
            normalize/   provider payload -> CanonicalSnapshot
            schedule/    slot plans in series timezone, idempotency keys
            analysis/    claim extraction, embeddings, clustering, recurrence
            metrics/     trigger, stability, survival, format, GOA, drift, diff
            fetch/       etiquette fetcher, robots, Content-Signal, SSRF guard
            pages/       structural features, platform class, page analysis
            brief/       coverage matrix, brief schema, export with AI marker
            match/       own-page matcher, section matcher
            llm/         Anthropic client, batch helper, schemas, model aliases
            db/          Drizzle schema, forOrg/withOrg helpers, RLS
            storage/     R2 put/get, lifecycle keys

Managed services: Neon Postgres + pgvector | Inngest | Stripe | Resend | PostHog | Sentry | Cloudflare R2 + Turnstile
Vendors:          DataForSEO (primary) | SerpApi (fallback, Legal Shield) | Anthropic | Voyage AI | Firecrawl (page-fetch fallback)
```

Data flow: add query, pre-check and confirm, join or create series, hourly scheduler enqueues due local slots with an idempotency key, DataForSEO task batch (100 per POST) with postback, postback stores raw JSON in R2 and emits one event, normalise into snapshot rows, content-hash and near-duplicate gate, hourly batch assembler sends unique snapshots to Claude in one Message Batch, clusters and metrics recomputed in SQL, daily diff email, readiness gate (preliminary at 3 capture-days, full at 5 capture-days and 10 renders), cited-page fetch and features, coverage matrix, brief, publish flow, matcher and section matcher on every new render, win and loss events.

Specs: `docs/spec/data-model.md`, `docs/spec/pipeline.md`, `docs/spec/metrics.md`, `docs/spec/brief-schema.md`, `docs/spec/own-page-matcher.md`.

## 5. Milestones and tasks

Each task is sized for one Claude Code session and names the files to create, the behaviour, and the acceptance test. Calendar estimate is 8 to 10 weeks; Milestone 0 and the beta cohort in Milestone 2 are calendar-gated by real capture days, so overlap them with the next milestone's non-dependent tasks.

### Milestone 2: the seven-day loop UI (week 5, beta calendar-gated by 7 capture days)

## 6. Risks and what the plan does about them

- **The data supply is in court.** Google v. SerpApi and Reddit v. SerpApi are live; DataForSEO's terms could be read against this use. Mitigation: never touch google.com, two providers behind one interface, SerpApi Production for the Legal Shield, written confirmation requested from DataForSEO, a vendor-shutdown runbook, derived-data-only outward surfaces, and a legal-risk register reviewed monthly.
- **AI Overviews are stochastic.** A 70 percent change probability between observations and 45 percent citation churn make single renders noisy. Mitigation: week-one double renders, Pro's 3 renders per day, survival buckets, sample sizes and confidence labels on everything, minimum evidence before win or loss emails, and a product-owned canary set to separate Google model changes from customer losses.
- **Extraction, not scraping, is the cost driver.** Mitigation: hash and near-duplicate short-circuits, the cheapest model tier for extraction, batch pricing, per-org budgets, and a re-measured cost model before pricing is locked.
- **Signed-out capture under-counts e-commerce overviews**, which are exactly the thread's target. Mitigation: the pre-check with sibling suggestions, the "watching" state, conservative onboarding copy, and vertical trigger-rate expectations.
- **Model lifecycle.** Haiku 4.5's retirement is not sooner than 2026-10-15. Mitigation: aliases only, prompt and schema versions stamped on every derived row, thresholds generated by the eval runner.
- **Platform fragility.** Google changed the overview layout four times in 2025 and 2026. Mitigation: fixture corpus, unknown block types preserved, schema failures feeding provider health, canary alerts, and a runbook for re-fixturing.
- **The brief lands on day 7 but trials decide earlier.** Mitigation: day-0 live render, daily digest from day 1, preliminary patterns at day 3, the Playbook as a mid-trial purchase.
- **Legiit's audience is seller-skewed.** Mitigation: a Playbook gig that sellers can resell, seller recruitment for build-from-brief gigs, and one external newsletter placement.
